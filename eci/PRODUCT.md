# ECI website

## Platform
Web. English only. Responsive company overview and product catalogue.

## Confirmed product truth
ECI / E&C Internetwork Co., Ltd. is primarily a distributor of defence and security products, not a manufacturer. Based in Bangkok, Thailand. Clients need to understand the company, see what it sells, and contact the team.

## Scope
Top navigation: About, Products, Solutions, Contacts. Home via the existing red ECI logo. Products has Air, Naval, Land, Software, Search and Rescue and All products. Each domain has an initial category with two clearly marked placeholder products. Category and product pages contain images and details. Solutions combines consultation, system design, installation and maintenance.

## Content and contact
Use existing logo and original photography plus relevant sourced photos to be replaced later. Do not imply representative internet photos depict products actually sold. No invented specifications, manufacturers, performance claims, certifications or company statistics. Existing address, email and both telephone numbers remain. Direct mailto/tel links assumed for initial version because the answer to contact options was ambiguous. No claimed message submission.

## Constraints
Blue and white with restrained red accents. Saab is a structural reference, not a template to copy. Simple content updates in source; no CMS, accounts, e-commerce or large menu.

## Product area clarification
Software covers programs for radar monitoring and identifying suspicious or potentially unlawful activity and threats. Search and Rescue covers locating and rescuing people at sea, in mountains and extreme terrain, including monitoring systems and transport/access platforms. Sample internet photographs illustrate these use cases; they do not establish specific product offers or partnerships.
