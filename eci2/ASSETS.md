# Asset provenance

ECI photographs, the original logo and partner logos were extracted from the user-supplied ECI_CompanyProfiles.pdf. See ASSETS-pdf.json for source pages and dimensions. Partner-logo display was explicitly approved. Source raster streams and masks were preserved; cropped partner marks retain their original proportions and colours. Web renditions use compressed imagery.

The original eci project supplies representative Air, Naval, Software and Search and Rescue imagery. Credits and source links are listed on /image-credits. The Land image was supplied by ECI's original public website. All catalogue entries remain marked as samples; no model availability or manufacturer relationship is inferred from these images.

Barlow fonts: Jeremy Tribby, SIL Open Font License; public/fonts/OFL.txt. The red corporate mark on the header/footer comes from PDF page 1.

## Solutions & Services catalogue

54 source images from company-profile pages 14–42 are stored in `public/images/catalogue/`. `ASSETS-catalogue.json` records their page and subject provenance.
